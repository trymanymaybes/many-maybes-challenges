# Challenge 06: What is this made of?

Keep all three original wavy lines visible and in their relative positions. Add clues that suggest a material without writing its name inside the picture. Whole-starter rotation and uniform resizing are allowed; moving individual lines is not.

Try three materials, then develop one with two visual clues.

- `starter.py`: the fixed starting marks.
- `fabric.py`: rail, rings and a hem suggest hanging cloth.
- `water.py`: banks, a boat and ripples suggest a river.
- `wood.py`: rings, a knot and roots suggest wood.

These are illustrative possibilities. Your idea can be completely different.
Run with Python's standard-library Turtle on a desktop, or paste the code into the Many Maybes playground in editable-code mode (choose Replace when switching starter modes). Do not also display the image starter if drawing the complete example.
